Frequently asked questions

How long does deposit approval take?

Deposits are typically reviewed and approved shortly after payment confirmation is submitted.

Can I withdraw my savings?

Withdrawal features are part of our roadmap — for now, KEPTO focuses on building consistent savings habits.

Will KEPTO support M-Pesa directly?

Yes — direct M-Pesa integration is planned so deposits can be confirmed automatically in the future.
