Frequently asked questions

Who can join KEPTO?

Any student interested in building a savings habit and exploring entrepreneurship can join.

How much do I need to start saving?

There's no fixed minimum — KEPTO is built around consistent small deposits, not large lump sums.

Is my money safe?

Deposits go through a verified confirmation and approval process before your balance updates. See the Savings page for details.
